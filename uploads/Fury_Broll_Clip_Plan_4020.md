# FQ-44 Fury B-roll and Animation Plan

Target: 16:9, 24 fps, 5-8 seconds per generated clip. Use official footage for factual proof points and AI animation for conceptual transitions, atmosphere, scale, and explanatory visuals.

## Visual system

- Palette: graphite black, cool steel, restrained warm amber accents.
- Camera: premium aerospace documentary; deliberate motion, long-lens compression, physically plausible aircraft movement.
- Fury continuity: long pointed nose, swept wings, single vertical tail, matte dark-gray finish, no cockpit.
- Avoid generated text, logos, flags, fictional insignia, invented weapons, cockpit views inside Fury, morphing geometry, extra wings, duplicated aircraft, and explosions unless the script explicitly calls for combat illustration.
- Add titles, labels, radar UI, numbers, and logos in the edit, not in the generator.

## Editorial notes

- Keep AI shots short. Cut before geometry drift becomes visible.
- Reuse the same approved Fury source image for reference-led shots.
- Let real footage carry evidence; let generated footage carry mood, scale, and concepts.
- For jokes such as the gamer gag or Terminator reference, use licensed material or an original parody graphic rather than generated lookalikes of copyrighted footage.
- Maintain one consistent grade across real and generated footage: slightly cool shadows, neutral highlights, restrained amber practicals.
